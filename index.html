As a general service interval, the AC can be checked at least once a year, or earlier if cooling performance drops, airflow weakens or you notice a refrigerant-related issue.

A comprehensive service can include refrigerant evacuation, cleaning, moisture and trapped-oil removal, HVAC inspection, compressor-oil checks or top-up, and gas-leak testing depending on the service requirement.

The exact cost depends on the fault, vehicle and parts or service required. Contact our team for an estimate after explaining the issue.

One common sign is that the AC stops blowing sufficiently cold air. A proper inspection can confirm whether the issue is low refrigerant, leakage or another AC component.

Because car AC systems involve refrigerant, pressure, electrical and mechanical components, it is best to have the issue inspected by a trained technician rather than attempting an uncertain repair.
